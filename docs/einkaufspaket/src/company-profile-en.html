Compliance and financial-crime training for every employee

RALK is an online training platform that trains every employee to recognise fraud, corruption, sanctions breaches, money laundering, data leaks and cyber attacks, tailored to their role, sector and country, and documents completion with certificates anyone can verify.

What RALK offers: 9 modules

Every employee completes all 9 modules. Scenarios, red flags and examples adapt to each learner’s role, sector and country. The reference framework is the FATF standards and applicable regulation.

1Fraud prevention
2Anti-bribery & corruption
3Sanctions & embargo
4AML/CFT, KYC & PEP
5Data protection
6Cybersecurity
7AI & financial crime
8Crypto & virtual assets
9Whistleblower protection

The AML/CFT module covers KYC, beneficial owners, politically exposed persons and suspicious transaction reports.

Who it is for

Every organisation exposed to financial crime. Regulated firms must train their staff by law; companies that work with banks, international partners or public buyers need proof of compliance too.

  • Banks and microfinance institutions
  • Insurers, asset managers and brokers
  • Fintechs, payment service providers, e-money and mobile money
  • Mining, oil & gas, telecoms and energy
  • Construction, trade and other corporates
  • NGOs, public bodies and professional associations

How it works

  • Licence: choose the number of employees, accept the licence terms online, pay by card via a secure link.
  • Staff import: upload the employee list; each person gets their own access.
  • Role-based modules: short modules with a real-life scenario and an assessment, on any device.
  • Verifiable certificates: each completion produces a certificate with a QR code that can be verified online.
  • Manager dashboard: progress per team and module, reports for management, partners, auditors and supervisors.
  • Languages: English and French.
