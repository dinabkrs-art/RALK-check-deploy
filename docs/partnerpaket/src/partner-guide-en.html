Recommend RALK, earn 10 %

You recommend RALK to organisations in your network. They order online at fixed public prices. RALK takes care of everything else. You receive 10 % of the first licence year.

How it works

1. RecommendSend the sample message below, or the link ralk.io/order?partner=CODE, to the organisations you know.
2. The client ordersThe organisation orders at ralk.io/order. With your link, your partner code is filled in automatically; otherwise the client enters it in the field "Partner code".
3. RALK deliversRALK confirms the order, sends the invoice with a secure card payment link, activates access after payment and supports the client. No presentations or negotiations are needed on your side.
4. You are paidOnce RALK has received the client's payment, your commission is paid within 30 days after the end of that month by bank transfer.

Who is RALK for?

Banks, microfinance institutions, payment and mobile money providers, insurers and companies with compliance obligations, in particular in sub-Saharan Africa. RALK trains every employee on fraud, anti-bribery, sanctions, AML/CFT, data protection, cybersecurity, AI and financial crime, crypto-assets and whistleblowing, adapted to role, sector and country, with certificates and reports for supervisors and correspondent banks.
