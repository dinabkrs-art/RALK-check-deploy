1. Purpose

The Parties intend to cooperate under the RALK partner programme, in which the Partner recommends the RALK training platform to organisations (the "Purpose"). For this Purpose they may exchange confidential information.

2. Confidential information

Confidential information means all information that a Party discloses to the other in connection with the Purpose, in any form, which is marked as confidential or is confidential by its nature. This includes in particular the terms of the partner programme, client and prospect information, access data for demonstration or trial accounts, non-public product information and business plans. Prices published at ralk.io are not confidential.

3. Obligations

3.1 The receiving Party uses confidential information only for the Purpose and protects it with at least the care it applies to its own confidential information, and in any case with reasonable care.

3.2 It discloses confidential information only to employees and advisers who need it for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as strict as those in this Agreement.

3.3 Personal data are exchanged only where this is lawful, and each Party complies with the data protection law that applies to it.

4. Exceptions

The obligations do not apply to information that (a) is or becomes publicly available without breach of this Agreement, (b) was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure, (c) is developed independently by the receiving Party, (d) is lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality, or (e) must be disclosed by law or by order of a court or authority; in that case the receiving Party informs the other Party in advance where legally permitted.

5. Return and deletion

On request, and in any case at the end of the cooperation, the receiving Party returns or deletes the confidential information, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

6. No further obligations

This Agreement grants no licence or other rights to the confidential information and does not oblige either Party to enter into any further agreement.

7. Term

This Agreement applies from signature. The obligations under sections 3 to 5 continue for three years after the end of the cooperation.

8. Change of address and succession

8.1 A change of RALK's address or registered seat does not affect this Agreement. RALK informs the Partner of the new address in text form.

8.2 RALK may transfer this Agreement with all rights and obligations to a successor entity that continues the RALK business, in particular a company founded or controlled by Dina Boyele Kraus (for example a GmbH). The Partner hereby consents to such a transfer. It takes effect on written notice to the Partner, and the Partner's rights remain unchanged.

9. Final provisions

9.1 This Agreement is governed by the law of the Federal Republic of Germany. Place of jurisdiction is the registered seat of RALK at the time the action is brought.

9.2 Amendments require text form (for example e-mail). Electronic signatures are sufficient.

9.3 If the English and French versions differ, the English version prevails. Should any provision be invalid, the remaining provisions remain unaffected.
