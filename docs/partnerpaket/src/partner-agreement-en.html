1. Subject

1.1 The Partner recommends RALK licences (online compliance training for employees, ralk.io) to organisations in its network. RALK pays the Partner a commission for licences ordered by organisations referred by the Partner ("Referred Clients").

1.2 The cooperation is non-exclusive. The Partner acts independently and is not an agent, employee or representative of RALK. The Partner has no authority to make declarations on behalf of RALK or to conclude contracts for RALK.

1.3 Contracts with Referred Clients are concluded exclusively between RALK and the client on the basis of the RALK Licence Terms. RALK handles ordering, invoicing, set-up and support.

2. Partner code

2.1 RALK assigns the Partner the personal partner code CODE and the partner link ralk.io/order?partner=CODE.

2.2 An order counts as referred by the Partner if the partner code is entered in the order at ralk.io/order or the client names the Partner in writing to RALK before ordering.

2.3 No commission is due if the organisation was already a RALK customer, or had already ordered, requested a trial or been in documented contact with RALK in the six months before the order. RALK informs the Partner of this within 14 days of the order. If several partner codes are named, the first one entered counts.

3. Commission

3.1 The commission is 10 % of the net licence fee actually received by RALK for the first licence year of a Referred Client. Net means excluding value added tax and other taxes, withholding tax, and card, bank or payment fees. Renewals and later orders do not give rise to a commission unless agreed otherwise in writing.

3.2 The commission becomes due once RALK has received the client's full payment. RALK pays it within 30 days after the end of the month in which the payment was received, by bank transfer to the account named by the Partner, on the basis of an invoice from the Partner or a credit note issued by RALK.

3.3 If RALK has to refund the licence fee in whole or in part, or the payment is reversed (for example a card chargeback), the commission is reduced accordingly; commissions already paid may be offset or are repaid.

3.4 The Partner is responsible for its own taxes and duties on the commission. Bank fees on the Partner's side are borne by the Partner.

3.5 On request RALK informs the Partner of the status of orders made with its code.

4. Partner obligations

4.1 The Partner presents RALK accurately and uses the information and materials provided by RALK. It makes no promises about services, prices or results beyond those published by RALK. Prices are fixed public prices; the Partner grants no discounts.

4.2 The Partner complies with all applicable anti-bribery and anti-corruption laws. In particular, it neither offers nor grants any payment or other advantage to employees, officers or public officials in connection with a recommendation.

4.3 The Partner is itself responsible for ensuring that this cooperation and the commission are compatible with the professional rules that apply to it, and for any disclosure to clients that these rules require.

5. Name and materials

RALK grants the Partner a non-exclusive, revocable right, limited to the term of this Agreement, to use the RALK name, logo and the materials provided for recommending RALK. The materials may not be altered.

6. Confidentiality and data protection

The Parties' non-disclosure agreement applies. Each Party complies with the data protection law that applies to it. The Partner passes on contact details of third parties only where this is lawful.

7. Term and termination

7.1 This Agreement applies from signature for an indefinite period. Either Party may terminate it with 30 days' notice in text form (for example e-mail). The right to terminate for good cause remains unaffected, in particular in the event of a breach of section 4.2.

7.2 Commissions for orders placed before the end of this Agreement remain due under section 3.

8. Liability

Each Party is liable for its own conduct. RALK is liable without limitation for intent and gross negligence; in all other cases RALK's liability is limited to the commission paid or payable under this Agreement in the twelve months before the claim.

9. Change of address and succession

9.1 A change of RALK's address or registered seat does not affect this Agreement. RALK informs the Partner of the new address in text form.

9.2 RALK may transfer this Agreement with all rights and obligations to a successor entity that continues the RALK business, in particular a company founded or controlled by Dina Boyele Kraus (for example a GmbH). The Partner hereby consents to such a transfer. It takes effect on written notice to the Partner; the Partner's rights, including commissions already earned, remain unchanged.

10. Final provisions

10.1 This Agreement is governed by the law of the Federal Republic of Germany. Place of jurisdiction is the registered seat of RALK at the time the action is brought.

10.2 Amendments require text form (for example e-mail). Electronic signatures are sufficient.

10.3 If the English and French versions differ, the English version prevails. Should any provision be invalid, the remaining provisions remain unaffected.
