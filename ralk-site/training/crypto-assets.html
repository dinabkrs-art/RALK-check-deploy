Why it matters

FATF Recommendation 15 brings virtual assets and virtual asset service providers under AML/CFT rules, including the travel rule for transfers. Banks and payment providers see customers move funds to and from exchanges, and investment scams promising crypto returns target customers and staff alike.

Who it is for
