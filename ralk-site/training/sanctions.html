Why it matters

FATF Recommendations 6 and 7 require countries to implement targeted financial sanctions without delay, including those of the United Nations Security Council. Banks and international partners expect their counterparties to screen and freeze correctly. Sanctions lists change constantly, so training has to stay current.

Who it is for
