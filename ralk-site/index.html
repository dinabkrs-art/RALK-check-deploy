Why RALK

FAQ

Questions before you start

How long does the training take?

Each employee completes short modules of about 10 minutes, each with a real-life scenario and a short test. Training can be paused and continued at any time, on any device.

Which topics are covered?

13 risk areas: fraud, bribery and corruption, sanctions, money laundering, data protection, cybersecurity, AI-enabled crime, whistleblowing and more. Each employee receives the modules that matter for their role, sector and country.

Is the content adapted to our company?

Yes. RALK tailors every training to your sector, your country's rules and each learner's role. A cashier, a buyer and a board member see different situations.

How do we prove the training to auditors and supervisors?

Every completion produces a certificate with a QR code that anyone can verify online. Management sees progress per team and module and can export reports for audits and inspections.

Does the training need to be repeated?

Certificates are valid for 12 months. With each annual licence your staff complete a refreshed training that includes new rules, sanctions and typologies.

How do employees get access?

You import your staff list or connect your HR system. Employees sign in with your company code or with their Microsoft or Google work account. Nothing needs to be installed.

Which languages are available?

English and French. Further languages on request.

How is our data protected?

RALK processes only the data needed for training and certificates, under the GDPR and a data processing agreement with your organisation. Access is restricted to your administrators.

Can we try RALK first?

Yes. The free 10-minute compliance check shows where your organisation stands and which modules your teams need.

How do we pay?

By invoice and bank transfer in USD (or EUR on request). Invoices are due within 14 days. Access is activated on payment or, for public bodies, on receipt of the purchase order.

How long does the licence run?

12 months. It renews automatically unless you cancel at least 30 days before the end of the term.

Can we add employees later?

Yes. Additional seats can be booked at any time at the rate of your size band.

Can we negotiate the price?

No. Like Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, RALK uses one published price list for everyone. Volume discounts are already included in the size bands.

Is RALK legal advice?

No. RALK provides training. It supports, but does not replace, your compliance function and legal advice.
