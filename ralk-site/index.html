FAQ

Questions before you start

How do we pay?

By invoice and bank transfer in USD (or EUR on request). Invoices are due within 14 days; access is activated on payment or, for public bodies, on receipt of the purchase order.

What about withholding tax?

Prices are net. If withholding tax or a similar levy applies in your country, it is borne by you in addition to the price, so that RALK receives the full invoiced amount.

Do we need anyone on site?

No. Everything runs online: ordering, onboarding, training and reporting. Our team supports you by email or video call.

Which languages are available?

English and French. Further languages on request.

Can we add employees later?

Yes. Additional seats can be booked at any time at the rate of your size band.

Can we negotiate the price?

No. Like Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, RALK uses one published price list for everyone. Volume discounts are already included in the size bands, so every customer gets the same fair price.

Is RALK legal advice?

No. RALK provides training. It supports, but does not replace, your compliance function and legal advice.
