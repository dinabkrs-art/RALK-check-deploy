2. What our services do

check.ralk.io — A free, browser-based self-assessment tool. It calculates an AML/FATF risk profile entirely within your browser. No assessment answers are transmitted to any server. A score and prioritised action plan are generated locally and displayed to you.

app.ralk.io — A SaaS training platform for compliance teams. It generates customised AML/CFT training programmes by role, country, and risk profile, and tracks completion, quiz results, and certificates for audit purposes.

3. What data we process

3a. Quick-Check (check.ralk.io)
When you choose to unlock your full report via the optional lead-capture form, we process:

  • Full name, business email address, job title / role
  • Company / institution name, country, sector
  • Assessment result: overall score, risk classification, number of critical gaps
  • Submission timestamp

Your individual question responses are not transmitted to our servers and are not retained beyond your browser session.

3b. Training Platform (app.ralk.io)
When you create an account or are enrolled by your organisation, we process:

  • Full name and business email address
  • Job title / role, department, seniority level
  • Organisation name, country, sector
  • Login method (Google OAuth, Microsoft OAuth, or email magic link)
  • Training records: modules completed, quiz scores, pass/fail status, timestamps
  • Certificates issued, including issuance date and training content summary
  • AI-generated training content (personalised to your profile — not stored as personal data beyond your training record)

We also process standard server log data (IP address, browser type, date and time of access) across all RALK services.

4. Legal basis

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — for the Quick-Check lead form (voluntary, with explicit checkbox).
  • Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) — for Training Platform accounts: processing is necessary to provide the service you or your organisation subscribed to.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — server log data for security and abuse prevention across all services.

You can use the Quick-Check without providing personal data by skipping the form.

5. How we use your data

  • To deliver and personalise the training programme (app.ralk.io)
  • To track completion and generate auditable training certificates
  • To enable compliance managers to monitor team training status
  • To send you Quick-Check results and relevant RALK content (check.ralk.io)
  • To contact you about a product walkthrough or demo if you requested one
  • To improve and develop our services

We will never sell your data to third parties or use it for unrelated marketing.

6. Hosting and subprocessors

We use the following vendors to operate our services. All handle personal data under appropriate safeguards (standard contractual clauses / adequacy decisions where applicable):

  • Vercel Inc. (USA) — frontend hosting for check.ralk.io and app.ralk.io
  • Railway (USA) — backend API hosting for app.ralk.io
  • MongoDB Atlas / MongoDB Inc. (USA) — database for Training Platform user accounts, training records, and certificates
  • Anthropic PBC (USA) — AI model API used to generate personalised training content (content is generated on request; we do not send personal data beyond role/country/sector to generate content)
  • OpenAI / DALL·E (USA) — AI image generation for training scenario illustrations
  • Cloudflare R2 (Cloudflare Inc., USA) — storage of generated training images
  • Resend (USA) — transactional email delivery (magic links, training notifications)
  • FormSubmit.co — relay for Quick-Check lead form submissions to our inbox

We aim to conclude data processing agreements (Art. 28 GDPR) with all vendors handling personal data.

7. Cookies and browser storage

We use only technically necessary browser storage (session state, authentication tokens) to operate our services. We do not set tracking or marketing cookies on any RALK service.

8. Retention

Training Platform account data is retained for the duration of your organisation's subscription and deleted within 30 days of account closure on request. Quick-Check lead data is retained only as long as necessary for the purposes above. Server log data is retained according to our hosting providers' standard policies.
